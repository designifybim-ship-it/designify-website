Designify Policies

Clear expectations from enquiry to final delivery.

These policies explain how projects, payments, cancellations, ownership and client information are handled.

Service Agreements & Payment

Before work begins, the agreed project or package scope, deliverables, revision allowance, timeline and payment terms are confirmed in a Designify service agreement. A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to secure project-based work and begin production. Remaining balances are due according to the agreed project terms. Rush requests may attract an additional rush fee depending on the scope and required turnaround.

Revisions & Scope

Revisions are provided according to the service or package selected. Requests that materially change the approved scope may require a revised quote, timeline or additional fee.

Cancellation

If a project is terminated before completion, the Client agrees to pay a 50% kill fee of the total invoice amount to cover time and resources already committed.

Ownership & Portfolio Rights

Full copyright of final designs is transferred to the Client only after full payment has been received. Designify retains the right to showcase completed work in its professional portfolio, website and social media for promotional purposes.

Refund Policy

Deposits are non-refundable because they reserve project time and cover work already committed. Any other refund consideration will depend on the circumstances of the project and work completed.

Privacy

Information submitted through Designify forms or correspondence is used to respond to enquiries, manage projects and provide requested services. Client information is not intentionally sold to third parties. Where third-party services are used to operate the website or process communications, their own privacy practices may also apply.

Contact

Questions about these policies can be sent to designifybim@gmail.com.

Project Operations

Payments, timelines and support.

Payment Methods

Designify accepts CIBC FirstCaribbean 1stPay, bank transfer and cash where appropriate. Payment details are provided privately after a project is confirmed.

Turnaround & Rush Work

Project timelines are confirmed before work begins. Rush requests may carry an additional fee depending on the requested deadline and current workload.

Revisions & Scope

Your agreement states the revision allowance included in the project. Additional revisions or requests outside the confirmed scope may require an additional fee.

Website & Digital Aftercare

Post-launch support, website updates and ongoing digital assistance are available where included in the original scope or booked as additional support.
