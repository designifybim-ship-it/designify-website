How It Works

A straightforward process from first message to final files.

You will know what is being designed, what I need from you, when revisions happen and what is delivered at the end.

01

Send Your Enquiry

Share what you need, your business goals, preferred services, timeline and budget through the project form.

02

Scope & Quote

Your request is reviewed so the deliverables, turnaround, revision allowance and final price can be confirmed clearly.

03

Contract & Deposit

Your service agreement is signed and the required deposit is paid before the project officially enters the design queue.

04

Design & Revisions

Designify develops the work, shares drafts and completes the revision rounds included in your agreed project scope.

05

Final Delivery

Once the balance is cleared, final files are prepared and delivered in the formats outlined in your service agreement.
