DESIGNIFY MULTI-PAGE WEBSITE

Open index.html to start.

Pages are separate HTML files: Home, About, Services, Pricing, Packages, Portfolio, Reviews, Contact and Policies. Each portfolio business also has its own page inside /projects. 404.html is shown for any broken or missing link.

IMPORTANT: Keep the entire folder together. The HTML pages use the /assets folder for logos, CSS, JavaScript and portfolio images. The site's styles are in assets/canvas.css and its animations in assets/canvas.js.


EASY EDITS (all in assets/site.js, at the very top)

Social links: edit the DESIGNIFY_LINKS block. Paste the full Instagram, Facebook and Behance URLs there. A button with no URL stays hidden, so the Behance button appears as soon as you add its link. WhatsApp is already set to +1 (246) 230-1679.

Booking months: change DESIGNIFY_BOOKING_MONTHS (for example 'January & February'). Every "Bookings are open" line on the site updates.

Holiday special: the DESIGNIFY_PROMO block controls the 15% offer card that slides in once per visitor, and the offer line on the homepage holiday card. It switches itself off after endDate. Set enabled:false to turn it off early, or change the wording and give it a new code for a new offer (a new code shows the card again to returning visitors). Claims arrive in the Contact form with the offer noted.


OTHER NOTES

Cookie notice: Google Analytics only stores cookies after a visitor clicks Accept. Visitors can change their choice from "Cookie Settings" in the footer.

Free checklist: assets/designify-brand-checklist.pdf is offered to people who join the mailing list on the homepage.

Share image: assets/images/designify-share.jpg is the picture shown when the site is shared on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook.

404 page: 404.html uses <base href="/designify-website/"> so its links work from any address. If the site moves to its own domain, change that to <base href="/">.

Photo: the About page and the homepage "Hi, I'm Ariel" section show a "Photo coming soon" placeholder until a photo is added.
