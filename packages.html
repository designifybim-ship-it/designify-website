What counts as a design request?

A request means one standard design deliverable, such as a social post, flyer or single business collateral item. Larger work, including full website builds, full brand identity projects or multi-page publications, is scoped separately or may count as more than one request. This is confirmed before the month begins.

FAQs

Questions about packages?

Here are the things clients usually want to know before starting a project or monthly package.

Do I need to sign a contract?+

Yes. Once your scope is confirmed, Designify provides a service agreement outlining deliverables, revisions, payment terms, timelines and cancellation terms before work begins.

How does the 50% deposit work?+

For project-based work, a 50% non-refundable deposit secures your project and allows work to begin. Any remaining balance follows the payment terms in your agreement.

What happens if I cancel a project?+

If a project is terminated before completion, a 50% kill fee of the total invoice amount applies to cover time and resources already committed, as outlined in Designify's Policies and your service agreement.

How does Build Your Own Custom Package work?+

The preset plans are based on a set number of standard design requests each month. The custom builder is for clients who need a specific mix of recurring services. Its per-item rates are lower than one-off pricing because the estimate assumes ongoing monthly work. Fixed support services are charged once per month, quantity-based services are multiplied by the amount selected, and a 10% bundle saving appears at 5 monthly units. Revision rounds are a preference in the estimator and are confirmed in the final agreement.

Can I change the services in my monthly package?+

Yes, changes can be discussed before a new billing period. If the scope changes, your monthly price and agreement may need to be updated.

Are the package prices final?+

Displayed package prices are starting prices. The final cost is confirmed once Designify reviews your scope, request volume and turnaround needs.

Can clients outside Barbados work with Designify?+

Yes. Designify can work remotely with clients outside Barbados. The currency switcher provides a reference conversion while final invoices confirm the agreed billing currency.

Do monthly packages include website creation?+

Full website creation is quoted separately because the scope can vary significantly. The custom monthly builder includes ongoing website content and update support for existing Wix or Shopify sites.
