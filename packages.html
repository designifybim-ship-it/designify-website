Questions about packages?

What clients usually ask before starting.

Do I need to sign a contract?+

Yes. Once your scope is confirmed, you’ll get an agreement covering deliverables, revisions, payment, timing and cancellation.

How does the 50% deposit work?+

A 50% non-refundable deposit books your project. The rest follows the terms in your agreement.

What happens if I cancel a project?+

If a project is cancelled before it’s finished, a 50% kill fee of the total invoice applies, as set out in the Policies and your agreement.

How does Build Your Own Custom Package work?+

Preset plans give you a set number of requests each month. The builder lets you pick specific services at lower monthly rates. Monthly services are charged once, per-item services by quantity, and you save 10% at 5 or more units. Revisions are confirmed in your agreement.

Can I change the services in my monthly package?+

Yes, before a new billing period. If the scope changes, your price and agreement are updated.

Are the package prices final?+

Prices shown are starting prices. Your final price is confirmed once I review what you need.

Can clients outside Barbados work with Designify?+

Yes, I work remotely with clients anywhere. Your invoice confirms the currency.

Do monthly packages include website creation?+

New websites are quoted separately. The custom builder covers ongoing updates for existing Wix or Shopify sites.
