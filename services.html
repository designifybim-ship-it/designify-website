Working With Designify

What working with Designify looks like.

A few practical details so you know how timelines, revisions, rush work and handoff are handled before you book.

Turnaround Times

Turnaround depends on the service, scope and current queue. Your expected delivery window is included with the quote so you can plan around it.

Revisions

Your quote or package confirms how many revision rounds are included. Additional revisions or major scope changes may be billed separately.

Rush Requests

Need something sooner? Rush work may be accepted depending on availability and may include an additional rush fee based on the deadline.
