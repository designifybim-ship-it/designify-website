Crave Box

Project details
IndustryFood & Hospitality
In this project2 designs
Creative directionBoldCompactCraveable

Crave Box is designed around quick impact. Strong orange-and-black branding, bold type and close product presentation make the food feel filling, immediate and ready to order. The layouts keep the offer uncomplicated so customers can understand what they are getting without digging through too much information. It has the same confident food-first energy associated with the wider Crave visual world, while the boxed-meal format gives this project its own focused personality.
