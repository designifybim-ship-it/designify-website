Food & Hospitality

Crave & Co

Creative Direction
BoldMoodyIndulgent

Crave & Co. has a darker, more indulgent personality. Black backgrounds, fiery orange accents, large headlines and rich close-up food photography give the brand a confident late-night energy that suits wings, loaded potatoes, combo meals and comfort food. The system is designed to make each offer feel substantial and satisfying while keeping pricing, flavours and calls to action easy to find. It feels bold without becoming messy, and every piece carries the same unmistakable Crave & Co. attitude.
