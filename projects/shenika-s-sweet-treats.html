Shenika's Sweet Treats

Creative Direction
SweetWelcomingPersonal

Shenika’s Sweet Treats takes a softer, more personal approach to dessert branding. The visual direction is intentionally welcoming, allowing the treats and the maker behind them to feel approachable while still presenting the business with a polished finish. The goal is not to make the brand feel overly corporate. Instead, it creates a friendly visual presence that customers can connect with quickly and that can grow naturally as the business adds more products and promotions.
