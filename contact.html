Questions before you get started?

Quick answers to what clients usually ask. Still unsure? Email designifybim@gmail.com or message on WhatsApp.

How long will my project take?+

Turnaround depends on the service, scope and current queue. Your expected delivery window is included with your quote, so you can plan around it before anything is booked.

Do you work with clients outside Barbados?+

Yes. Designify works remotely with clients outside Barbados. The currency switcher on the Pricing and Packages pages gives a quick reference, and your final invoice confirms the agreed billing currency.

What do you need from me to get started?+

Your final wording, business details, preferred images, brand assets, any inspiration you like and, for website or booking projects, platform login or access information when requested. Delays in receiving content can affect the timeline.

How does payment work?+

A 50% non-refundable deposit secures project-based work and lets design begin. Designify accepts CIBC FirstCaribbean 1stPay, bank transfer and cash where appropriate. Payment details are shared privately once your project is confirmed.

Can I get something done urgently?+

Rush requests may be accepted depending on availability and may carry an additional fee based on the deadline and current workload.

Do I need to sign a contract?+

Yes. Once your scope is confirmed, you’ll receive a service agreement covering deliverables, revisions, payment terms, timelines and cancellation terms before work begins. Sending an enquiry does not create a contract.
